# GDC capture checklist

Print `data/processed/gdc/print_pack.pdf` once on A4, black and white is fine, at 100 % scale (no 'fit to page'). Then capture every sheet as described below and save the files in `data/raw/gdc/captures/` (any file names; the corner squares identify each sheet).

Rules for every capture: all four black corner squares must be in the picture; no flash; keep the original image (no filters, no 'enhance').

## Conditions

- **scan_app** (37 sheets): Phone scanner app (Google Drive scan or Microsoft Lens), page flat, auto crop off or keep the whole sheet with all four corner squares visible.
- **photo_flat** (16 sheets): Plain phone photo from straight above, good daylight, sheet flat on a table.
- **photo_angled** (16 sheets): Phone photo at a clear angle (about 30-45 degrees), sheet flat.
- **low_light** (11 sheets): Phone photo in dim light or with a shadow across part of the sheet; no flash.
- **distant** (11 sheets): Phone photo from further away (sheet fills about a third of the frame).
- **rotated** (7 sheets): Sheet turned sideways or upside down on the table, photo from above.
- **curved** (8 sheets): Sheet held or bent slightly (like an open book page), photo from above.
