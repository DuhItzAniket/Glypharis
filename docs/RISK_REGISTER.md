| R-21 | **(Closed 2026-10-28, Phase 9: the trained kernels match the label width, median ratio 1.00; GW-DET-004 recalibrated the unclip on validation.)** **Thin-kernel unclip calibration.** With the DBNet shrink ratio r = 0.4 a line's label kernel is thin. The exact inverse of the shrink multiplies any excess thickness of the predicted region by 1/r² = 6.25, while DBNet's unclip ratio 1.5 assumes a region much thicker than the kernel. Which one fits depends on the trained probability map, which is unknown before training, and a wrong unclip mis-sizes boxes and costs F1@0.5. | M | M | Two pre-registered operating points (`dbnet15`, `inverse10`) scored from the same forward pass, with max selection on validation (EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL §7); a kernel-excess diagnostic on every validation pass; the `BOX SIZE MISMATCH` flag; the label ceiling gated on the selection metric before any GPU run; Phase 9 calibrates `bin_thresh` and `unclip_ratio` on validation from saved candidates and cached probability maps | `BOX SIZE MISMATCH` raised; the label-ceiling gate fails | 8, 9 |
| R-22 | **(Still open after Phase 9: HierText validation has no curved line annotations, so GW-DET-006 could not compare `poly` with `quad`; Phase 14 uses `quad`.)** **Curved GDC lines under a rectangle box output.** GDC, the Phase 14 test set where nothing may be tuned afterwards, has curved, densified line polygons from phone photos. A rectangle around a long curved line can fall below IoU 0.5, so detection would score worse than it reads, with no chance to fix it after the test. **Note (2026-11-17, Phase 15, GW-E2E-010 and GW-REC-040; the risk stays open):** on GDC's 8 `curved` pages (8 documents, `quad` boxes) the pipeline's 10,082 bag-of-lines errors are missed lines 54.95% [46.41, 64.46], matched lines 8.08% [1.73, 18.85] and spurious predictions 36.97% [26.42, 47.78] (document CIs), and recall at IoU 0.5 is 69.33% [58.17, 76.01]. From ground-truth boxes the recognizer makes 12.47% [0.71, 35.21] as many errors as the whole pipeline there; its crop CER on those lines is 8.07% [0.42, 24.16], and 0.26% (no CI) on the curved lines of the paper group, so the crop CER goes with the documents. Misread matched lines are a small part of the curved pages' error; the rest is lines not found and spurious boxes, the failure this risk describes. Whether `poly` boxes would recover them was not measured, and 8 pages from 8 documents cannot separate curvature from document (`reports/error_cases/phase15/pipeline.md`, `oracle_share.md`, `recognition.md`). | M | M | `det.post.box_type: poly` (the kernel contour offset by the same unclip distance) exists from Phase 8, and every saved candidate keeps its simplified contour, so Phase 9 compares `quad` and `poly` on validation, including its most curved lines, and fixes the Phase 14 setting before GDC is read. All GW-DET runs use `quad`, the rectangle Phase 10 rectifies. GDC-scan's pages curl at the binding too, so the risk covers it; Phase 14 reports `quad` only | Phase 9 finds `poly` ahead of `quad` on curved validation lines | 8, 9, 14 |
