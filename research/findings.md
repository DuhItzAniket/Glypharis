# Findings

Conclusions that are supported by at least one completed, recorded experiment.
Each finding cites the experiment IDs behind it and states its confidence.
A finding can be revised or withdrawn later; keep the history and say why.

| F1 | A CTC recognizer's validation loss can creep up late in training while its validation CER keeps improving, so validation-loss divergence alone is not evidence of overfitting. A rule built on it raised eight false alarms. | GW-REC-001 | medium (one run) | 2026-10-23 | current |
| F2 | Adding 35,000 GlyphSynth lines to HierText training did not hurt HierText validation CER (paired difference −0.05 points [−0.09, −0.01]). The gain is not attributable to the synthetic data: one seed, and 10% more optimizer steps. | GW-REC-002 vs GW-REC-001 | low | 2026-10-23 | current |
| F3 | For HierText line recognition at this model size, input height 48 helps most of any single change (−0.49 points on the short budget), and it combines with an SVTR-style encoder and weight decay 1e-2 to more than the sum of the three (−0.76 against −0.66). At the full budget the combination reaches 6.46% validation CER, -1.07 [-1.13, -1.01] points against the CRNN baseline. | GW-REC-006, 014, 020, 022, 024 vs GW-REC-001, 003 | medium (one seed per configuration) | 2026-10-26 | current |
| F4 | Halving the frames per line (frame stride 8) costs 2.6 points of CER on HierText lines: many lines are too dense for one frame per 8 input columns. Capacity cuts to the CRNN cost 0.46 to 0.51 points each. | GW-REC-021, 007, 008, 009 | medium (one seed each) | 2026-10-26 | current |
| F5 | On HierText, the trained detector's errors are mostly misses, not false alarms: precision 0.92, recall 0.59 against a 0.95 label ceiling. Recall falls to 0.20 for lines under 8 px high and to 0.18 for lines rotated beyond 45°, against 0.72 for 12–20 px horizontal lines. | GW-DET-001 | medium (one run, 74,500 lines) | 2026-10-27 | current |
| F6 | For the DBNet-style detector at 640² crops and a 24-epoch budget, ImageNet initialisation is worth about 0.02 F1@0.5 and MobileNetV3-Large over Small about 0.056, both mostly through recall; Small loses most on small text. | GW-DET-002, 003 vs GW-DET-001 | medium (one seed each) | 2026-10-27 | current |
| F7 | Of GW-DET-001's 30,184 missed validation lines, 46% had a candidate region that only the confidence threshold removed, 37% produced no candidate at all (mostly lines under 8 px high) and 11% were merged with a neighbour. Its predicted kernels match the label kernels' width (median ratio 1.00), so the exact-inverse unclip is the right regime. | GW-DET-001, `reports/error_cases/detector/GW-DET-001/` | medium (one run, 74,500 lines) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F8 | Post-processing chosen on half of validation and checked on the other half adds 0.032 F1@0.5 [0.025, 0.039] to the same network: a lower box threshold (0.5) and slightly smaller boxes trade seven points of precision for eight of recall. | GW-DET-004 | medium (one network, two halves of one set) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F9 | For GW-DET-001, input resolution is worth as much as the post-processing: 1536 px on the long side adds 0.018 F1@0.5 [0.013, 0.022] over 1280, and accuracy keeps rising to 2048; the CPU page-time budget, not accuracy, sets the limit. With both changes, validation F1@0.5 rises from 0.724 to 0.778 without retraining, at the cost of 2.5 times as many false alarms. | GW-DET-005, GW-EFF-001 | medium (one network) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F10 | On HierText validation the GLYPHARIS pipeline reads pages with 24.3% bag-of-lines CER against 42.4% for the best Phase 5 baseline (PP-OCRv6 small) on the same 400 pages, −18.1 points [−21.4, −15.1]; with ground-truth line boxes its CER would be 6.7%, so detection causes about three quarters of the remaining page error, on sharp and blurry pages alike. | GW-E2E-001, GW-E2E-002 | medium (one detector, one recognizer, HierText only) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F11 | The recognizer's raw line confidence is strongly over-confident (ECE 0.22 on validation lines); one temperature (2.35) fitted on half of validation brings it to 0.09 on the other half. | GW-E2E-003 | medium (one model, one dataset) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F12 | In PyTorch on the CPU, recognition dominates the page time (1.7 of 2.0 s for 41 lines) and costs about 42 ms per line in the pipeline, against 24 ms for single lines; Phase 9's per-line estimate was too optimistic. | GW-EFF-002, GW-EFF-001 | medium (one machine) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F13 | Exported to ONNX with the TorchScript exporter, the SVTR-style recognizer reads exactly like PyTorch but runs about 4.5 times slower on the CPU under ONNX Runtime, and the runtime's memory arena grows with every new input shape (11 GB over 110 pages); the exported DBNet detector runs twice as fast as PyTorch. Exporting does not by itself make a transformer-style recognizer faster on a laptop CPU. | GW-EFF-003, GW-EFF-004 | medium (one machine, one exporter and runtime version) | 2026-10-28 | current |
| F14 | On the CRNN short budget, geometric augmentation is the family that makes the recognizer robust: it lowers GLYPH-C mean corruption error by −1.67 [−1.73, −1.62] points against no augmentation and clean validation CER with it. Document degradation helps a little (−0.25 [−0.30, −0.19], from blur, low resolution and JPEG) without clean cost; photometric augmentation shows no clear effect; all three together are best (−1.83 [−1.90, −1.75]). | GW-DATA-009 (15 runs: GW-REC-003 to 005, 015 to 018, GW-DATA-001 to 008) | medium (three seeds per arm; one recipe, validation-derived corruptions) | 2026-10-29 | current |
| F15 | Small in-plane rotation is the recognizer's largest weakness: whole-line rotation of 1.2° to 6° raises CER to 38.89% without augmentation and 33.31% with the full preset (mean over the five levels), against 4.14% and 3.41% on the clean copies; training rotates by at most ±2°. Rotation accounts for more than half of the unweighted mCE. | GW-DATA-009 | medium (one recipe; the pipeline's box rectification removes page-level rotation, not rotation within a box) | 2026-10-29 | current |
| F16 | At a fixed step budget, adding GlyphSynth lines to HierText does not help the CRNN on HierText's dense, document-like pages, and hurts from 50K lines on: document-slice CER 11.06% with HierText only, 11.09% at +25K (no clear effect), 11.44% at +250K (+0.39 [+0.25, +0.52]). On GlyphSynth's own held-out-font lines CER falls from 1.97% to 0.38%: the model learns the rendered lines, but they do not transfer to photographed text. | GW-DATA-022 (GW-DATA-010 to 019, GW-REC-003 to 005) | medium (three seeds at 25K, 100K, 250K; HierText proxy slice, not a document set) | 2026-10-30 | current |
| F17 | GlyphSynth's capture effects (rotation, perspective, texture, lighting, blur, noise, JPEG) make the synthetic lines worth more for robustness: without them, GLYPH-C mCE is +0.31 [+0.27, +0.35] points higher at 100K lines, with no change on clean validation; restricting the fonts to 6 families made no clear difference. | GW-DATA-022 (GW-DATA-014 to 016, 020, 021) | low to medium (one seed per factor run) | 2026-10-30 | current |
