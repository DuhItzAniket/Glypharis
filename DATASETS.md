# Datasets

Provenance, licence and role of every dataset GLYPHARIS uses or considered. Roles are fixed by
[ADR-0005](docs/decisions/ADR-0005-evaluation-data-roles.md) and
[ADR-0009](docs/decisions/ADR-0009-no-synthtext.md). Machine-readable records are in
`data/DATASET_MANIFEST.yaml` and `data/manifests/*.json` (generated by `scripts/download_datasets.py`).
Datasets themselves are never committed.

Licences were checked on the official pages on **2026-10-10** (Phase 2). Counts marked "(published)" are
the authors' figures; counts marked "(measured)" come from the downloaded files via the manifests.

## Training-data rule

Every source that can influence the weights of a model delivered to a consumer must allow commercial use,
possibly with attribution or share-alike ([ADR-0009](docs/decisions/ADR-0009-no-synthtext.md)).
Research-only or unlicensed datasets are used **only for evaluation**.

## Dataset cards

Each card follows the brief's required fields: name, source, URL, licence, citation, download date,
version, samples, annotation format, intended task, preprocessing, split usage.

### HierText

- **Source / URL:** Google Research, https://github.com/google-research-datasets/hiertext (repository
  archived, read-only). Images hosted by CVDF in the Open Images bucket
  (`https://s3.amazonaws.com/open-images-dataset/ocr/{train,validation,test}.tgz`).
- **Citation:** Long et al., *Towards End-to-End Unified Scene Text Detection and Layout Analysis*,
  CVPR 2022 (`long2022hiertext`).
- **Licence:** the README states the dataset is released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The images come from Open
  Images, which lists them as CC BY 2.0 and makes no warranty about each image's licence status.
  Obligations: attribution; share-alike for the annotations and anything derived from them.
- **Version:** 2022 release; test annotations published 2024-12-02 (so HierText test has ground truth and
  the R-08 fallback is not needed). Image archives last modified 2022-03-03.
- **Integrity:** no published checksums. SHA-256 recorded on first download in
  `data/manifests/hiertext.json`. The three ground-truth files also match GitHub's git blob SHA-1
  (`git hash-object`), checked 2026-10-10.
- **Samples (published):** 11,639 images (train 8,281 / validation 1,724 / test 1,634), about 1.2M words.
- **Samples (measured, 2026-10-10, `data/manifests/hiertext.json`):**

  | Split | Images (files = annotated) | Paragraphs | Lines | Legible lines | Handwritten lines | Words | Legible words |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | train | 8,281 | 242,164 | 498,014 | 381,963 | 34,200 | 1,014,142 | 862,577 |
  | validation | 1,724 | 51,137 | 104,139 | 80,096 | 6,667 | 210,345 | 178,382 |
  | test | 1,634 | 46,316 | 94,564 | 73,737 | 7,103 | 194,374 | 167,188 |

  The published "about 1.2M words" matches the legible words (1,208,147); the files contain 1,418,861
  words in total. Downloaded size: 3.72 GiB archives + 79 MiB ground truth.
- **Annotation format:** gzipped JSON with paragraph → line → word hierarchy, polygon vertices,
  transcription, legibility and handwritten flags.
- **Intended task here:** detector training (line polygons), recognizer training (line crops), E2E
  validation; test split only in Phases 5 / 14 / 22.
- **Preprocessing (Phase 4):** illegible and handwritten lines excluded from recognizer training and marked
  "don't care" for detection; counts reported, nothing silently dropped.
- **Domain note:** mostly natural images containing text, with a share of document-like images. Not
  representative of scanned pages on its own (RISK_REGISTER R-06).

### GlyphSynth (ours, [ADR-0003](docs/decisions/ADR-0003-document-domain-synthetic-data.md))

- **What:** document-style text lines and pages rendered by `glypharis.data.synth` (Phase 4). The only
  synthetic training source ([ADR-0009](docs/decisions/ADR-0009-no-synthtext.md)).
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts (https://github.com/google/fonts), pinned to one commit and recorded in the
  font manifest. The top-level folder gives each font's licence (`ofl/` SIL OFL 1.1, `apache/` Apache 2.0,
  `ufl/` Ubuntu Font Licence). Fonts are used unmodified, so OFL Reserved Font Names are not an issue.
- **Text:** PG-19 (DeepMind, https://github.com/google-deepmind/pg19): pre-1919 Project Gutenberg books
  with the Gutenberg boilerplate removed, packaged under Apache 2.0, texts public domain in the US;
  public bucket with per-object MD5. Fetched through PG-19 rather than gutenberg.org, whose policy forbids
  automated access to the website. A small CC0 supplement (Standard Ebooks) is optional.
- **Degradations:** blur, noise, JPEG, low resolution, contrast, bleed-through, paper texture, skew,
  perspective, at controlled severities.
- **Splits:** GlyphSynth-val uses held-out fonts and held-out texts. GDC sources are excluded
  (EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL §1 rule 5).
- **Reproducibility:** fully determined by generator version, config, seed, font commit and text manifest.
- **Acquisition:** font and text sources are registered in `data/sources.yaml` in Phase 4, when the font
  list and text sample are designed.

### GDC — Glypharis Document Challenge (ours)

- **What:** a small, frozen, manually curated internal test set representing image-only document pages
  (brief §39, under a neutral name).
- **Composition target:** 100–150 pages covering clean scans, old scans, low resolution, photographed,
  rotated, skewed, multi-column, pages with figures and captions, number- and punctuation-heavy pages,
  low contrast.
- **Ground truth:** mainly print-and-capture from born-digital, openly licensed PDFs (exact text and line
  positions from the PDF, aligned to the captured image); a small manually transcribed subset of real old
  scans.
- **Rules:** built in Phase 4, frozen with a hash manifest before Phase 5, never trained on, sources on the
  training exclusion list, evaluated only in Phases 5, 14, 22.

### IIIT5K

- **Source:** CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad,
  https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/research/projects/cvit-projects/the-iiit-5k-word-dataset
- **Download:** `IIIT5K-Word_V3.0.tar.gz` (105,861,943 bytes per HEAD, 2014-03-19), no account, no
  checksums.
- **Citation:** Mishra, Alahari, Jawahar, BMVC 2012 (`mishra2012iiit5k`).
- **Licence:** none stated; the pages only ask for a citation. Treated as research use, evaluation only.
- **Samples (published):** 5,000 cropped words (2,000 train / 3,000 test per the paper; split sizes not
  stated on the site).
- **Usage:** test split only, as an out-of-domain word-level recognizer test (RQ6).

### FUNSD

- **Source:** https://guillaumejaume.github.io/FUNSD/ — `dataset.zip` (16,838,830 bytes per HEAD).
- **Citation:** Jaume, Ekenel, Thiran, ICDAR-OST 2019 (`jaume2019funsd`).
- **Licence:** custom EPFL licence, non-commercial research and educational use only; the scanned forms
  (from RVL-CDIP) remain under their own copyright. Using the data means accepting those terms, which the
  owner does before Phase 14.
- **Samples (published):** 199 forms (149 train / 50 test), 31,485 words.
- **Usage:** external E2E test on noisy scanned forms; evaluation only.

### OmniDocBench

- **Source:** https://github.com/opendatalab/OmniDocBench and
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/opendatalab/OmniDocBench (not gated).
- **Citation:** Ouyang et al., CVPR 2025 (`ouyang2025omnidocbench`).
- **Licence:** code Apache-2.0; the data is stated to be for research purposes only and not for commercial
  use. Evaluation only.
- **Version / samples (published):** 1,651 page images on the Hugging Face main branch (v1.6 content);
  pages tagged en / zh / mixed. Pin a commit in Phase 14 and count English pages from its JSON.
- **Integrity:** Hugging Face LFS SHA-256 per file.
- **Usage:** external document-domain test, English text blocks only.

### SROIE (optional)

- **Source:** ICDAR 2019 RRC, https://rrc.cvc.uab.es/?ch=13 — registration required; licence not visible
  publicly.
- **Citation:** Huang et al., ICDAR 2019 (`huang2019sroie`).
- **Samples (published):** 1,000 receipts. Low priority; decided in Phase 14.

### DocVQA (reference only)

- **Source:** https://rrc.cvc.uab.es/?ch=17 — registration required; terms behind login.
- **Citation:** Mathew, Karatzas, Jawahar, WACV 2021 (`mathew2021docvqa`).
- **Why reference only:** its OCR transcriptions were produced by a commercial OCR engine, not by humans
  ([ADR-0005](docs/decisions/ADR-0005-evaluation-data-roles.md), RISK_REGISTER R-19). Not downloaded.

### SynthText (dropped)

- **Source:** Visual Geometry Group, Oxford, https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/scenetext/
- **Citation:** Gupta, Vedaldi, Zisserman, CVPR 2016 (`gupta2016synthetic`).
- **Licence:** VGG "Dataset Group 2" terms of access: non-transferable licence strictly for
  non-commercial research, no use of outputs in commercial products, no redistribution, 12-month term
  after which the data must be deleted.
- **Availability:** the project page says the download links are no longer available.
- **Samples (published):** 858,750 images, about 7.3M words; one archive of about 41 GB.
- **Decision:** not downloaded or used ([ADR-0009](docs/decisions/ADR-0009-no-synthtext.md)). The brief's
  SynthText subset study is replaced by a GlyphSynth scaling study at the same sizes.

## Licensing implications

- Models delivered to a consumer are trained only on HierText and GlyphSynth. HierText's share-alike
  annotations mean the model card (Phase 21) must state attribution and how share-alike is handled for
  released weights.
- Evaluation-only datasets never enter training, augmentation or tuning.

## Consumer sample documents

Real documents from the consumer application are **not** a dataset of this repository. If the owner
provides sample pages for qualitative checks, they stay outside the repo (and outside git), are not used
for training or tuning, and only aggregate observations are written down.
